About

is a composer based in Los Angeles, crafting original music for the world's most ambitious brands. Every project is built from scratch, written with intention, shaped around the story, the image, and the emotion the work demands.

The result is music you won't hear anywhere else. Not because it's unconventional, but because it's built for one thing only: that specific film, that specific moment. Whether it's a sweeping orchestral score, a hard-hitting electronic track, or something that lives somewhere in between, every composition is designed to serve the work and leave a mark.

For the past four years, has collaborated closely with Concret Form across a wide range of projects, spanning composition, sound design, and music arrangement. That partnership has earned recognition from the biggest entertainment awards, including Bronze for Composition of the Year at the AICP Awards 2025 and Silver at the Shots Award.
